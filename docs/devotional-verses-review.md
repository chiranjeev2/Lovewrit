## 1. Hindu Tradition

### 1.1 Shanti Mantra
- **ID:** `hindu-shanti-mantra`
- **Faith:** Hindu
- **Language / Script:** Sanskrit (Devanagari script)
- **Source Reference:** *Brihadaranyaka Upanishad (1.4.14)*
- **Original Scripture:**
  ```text
  ॐ सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः।
  सर्वे भद्राणि पश्यन्तु मा कश्चिद्दुःखभाग्भवेत्।
  ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥
  ```
- **Transliteration:** `Om Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah Sarve Santu Niraamayaah | Sarve Bhadraanni Pashyantu Maa Kashcid-Duhkha-Bhaag-Bhavet | Om Shaantih Shaantih Shaantih ||`
- **Translation / Meaning:** May all sentient beings be happy; may all be free from illness; may all perceive what is auspicious and good; may no one experience suffering. Om Peace, Peace, Peace.
- **Verified:** `false` (Pending confirmation)

### 1.2 Shri Ganesha Vandana
- **ID:** `hindu-ganesha-vandana`
- **Faith:** Hindu
- **Language / Script:** Sanskrit (Devanagari script)
- **Source Reference:** *Traditional Sanskrit Invocative Stotram*
- **Original Scripture:**
  ```text
  वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ।
  निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा॥
  ```
- **Transliteration:** `Vakratunda Mahakaya Suryakoti Samaprabha | Nirvighnam Kuru Me Deva Sarvakaryeshu Sarvada ||`
- **Translation / Meaning:** O Lord with the curved trunk and magnificent form, radiant as ten million suns, please make all our endeavors free of obstacles, always.
- **Verified:** `false` (Pending confirmation)

### 1.3 Gayatri Mantra
- **ID:** `hindu-gayatri-mantra`
- **Faith:** Hindu
- **Language / Script:** Vedic Sanskrit (Devanagari script)
- **Source Reference:** *Rigveda (Mandala 3, Sukta 62, Verse 10)*
- **Original Scripture:**
  ```text
  ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं
  भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥
  ```
- **Transliteration:** `Om Bhur Bhuvah Svah Tat-Savitur-Varenyam | Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayaat ||`
- **Translation / Meaning:** We meditate on the supreme, adorable radiance of the Divine Creator; may that divine illumination inspire and guide our intellect and understanding.
- **Verified:** `false` (Pending confirmation)

## 2. Sikh Tradition

### 2.1 Mool Mantar
- **ID:** `sikh-mool-mantar`
- **Faith:** Sikh
- **Language / Script:** Gurmukhi
- **Source Reference:** *Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji (Ang 1)*
- **Original Scripture:**
  ```text
  ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥
  ```
- **Transliteration:** `Ik Oankar Sat Naam Karta Purakh Nirbhau Nirvair Akaal Moorat Ajoonee Saibhang Gur Parsaad ||`
- **Translation / Meaning:** One Universal Creator God; Truth is His Name; Creative Being Personified; Without Fear; Without Hatred; Timeless Form; Unborn and Beyond Incarnation; Self-Existent; Realized by the Grace of the True Guru.
- **Verified:** `false` (Pending confirmation)

### 2.2 Sukhmani Sahib • Peace & Healing

- **Faith:** Sikh
- **Language / Script:** Gurmukhi
- **Source Reference:** *Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji (Ang 274, Guru Arjan Dev Ji)*
- **Original Scripture:**
  ```text
  ਸਰਬ ਰੋਗ ਕਾ ਅਉਖਦੁ ਨਾਮੁ ॥ ਕਲਿਆਣ ਰੂਪ ਮੰਗਲ ਗੁਣ ਗਾਮ ॥
  ```
- **Transliteration:** `Sarab Rog Kaa Aoukhadh Naam || Kalyaan Roop Mangal Gun Gaam ||`
- **Translation / Meaning:** The Divine Name is the remedy for all afflictions; singing praises of the Almighty brings pure tranquility, auspicious joy, and ultimate salvation.
- **Verified:** `false` (Pending confirmation)

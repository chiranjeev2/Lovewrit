# 💌 Lovewrit Beta Testing Guide (Friend & Family Edition)

Thank you so much for helping test **Lovewrit**! We are building handcrafted digital greeting cards & emotional multimedia keepsake pages.

Please spend 5–10 minutes creating a test card or page on your mobile phone or laptop.

---

## 🎯 What to Try

1. **Pick an Occasion**:
   - Birthday, Anniversary, Romantic Proposal, Godhbharai (Baby Shower), Kitty Party, or Sacred Memorial.
2. **Customize Your Story**:
   - Write a short personal note.
   - Upload 1 or 2 test photos.
   - Try picking a music track or ambient background effect.
   - Change the color palette in Step 8 and see if the live preview reflects your choice.
3. **Try the Interactive Unboxing Features**:
   - **Birthday**: Blow out the birthday candle (tap/hold or microphone) or pop balloons.
   - **Godhbharai**: Scratch the golden due-date reveal card.
   - **Sacred Tribute**: Tap to light a memorial diya/candle. Note the unbranded solemn design.
   - **Kitty Party / Event**: Check out the venue map and RSVP form.
   - **Personalized Guest Links**: On event invites, try adding `?guest=YourName` to the URL.
4. **Try the Download Exports**:
   - On cards, test **Download PNG**, **Download JPG**, and **Foldable Card (PDF)**.
   - Check if the downloaded PDF opens cleanly on your phone or computer.
5. **Secret PIN (Optional)**:
   - Try setting a 4-digit PIN to lock your page and unlocking it on another tab.

---

## 🔍 What to Look Out For & Report

If you see anything off, please let us know:
- 📱 **Formatting on Your Device**: Did anything look squished, overlapping, or cut off on your phone model? (Please tell us your phone model e.g., iPhone 13, Samsung Galaxy S23, etc.)
- 🏃 **Speed & Smoothness**: Did scene transitions feel fluid, or was there any lag?
- 🎧 **Music / Audio**: Did background music play when you tapped to unmute?
- 🖨️ **Downloads**: Did the PDF look sharp and readable?
- 💭 **Copy & Emotion**: Did any wording feel awkward, confusing, or out of place?
